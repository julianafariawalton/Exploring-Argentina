Destinations

Argentina is a large country with many different places to visit. Here are three destinations that travelers may want to explore.

Buenos Aires

Buenos Aires is the capital of Argentina. Visitors can enjoy its historic neighborhoods, museums, restaurants, and cultural attractions.

Patagonia

Patagonia is located in southern Argentina. This region is famous for its mountains, lakes, glaciers, and outdoor activities.

Iguazu Falls

Iguazu Falls is located in northeastern Argentina, near the border with Brazil. Visitors can walk along trails and enjoy views of the waterfalls.
